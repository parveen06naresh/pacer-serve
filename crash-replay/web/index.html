How it works

Built like a real backtest, so your score means something.

01 · The rules

No peeking at tomorrow

You decide at each day's close and earn the next day's move. Each trade costs 0.05%. Cash earns the 3-month Treasury bill rate of the day, and S&P 500 positions earn dividends.

V(t+1) = V(t) · (1 + e·r_mkt + (1−e)·r_cash) e ∈ {0, ½, 1} cost = 5 bp · |Δe| · V

02 · Your opponents

The market and the quant

Buy and hold never trades. The quant runs the two rules trend-following funds use: stay invested only above the 200-day average, and size the bet to target 15% volatility.

size = 1[P > SMA200] · min(1, 15% / σ20) σ20 = stdev(20 daily log returns) · √252

03 · The skill test

Was it skill or luck?

2,000 random traders make exactly as many trades as you, on random days. If you beat 95% of them, your timing was better than chance. That is a permutation test, the same idea used to check trading strategies.

p-value ≈ share of random traders who finished above you
